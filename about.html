About Habiboil

A family-inspired mustard oil brand built on purity, care, and desi tradition.

Habiboil was created for Pakistani households that still believe the best food starts with honest ingredients. Our inspiration comes from traditional kitchens where meals are cooked with love, recipes are preserved, and trust matters most.

Our Story

From family breakfasts to festive daawats, mustard oil has always played a central role in our food culture. Habiboil honors that legacy by delivering a product that feels premium, tastes traditional, and speaks to the confidence of modern households.

Our Promise

We aim to combine a clean, modern experience with the emotional comfort of ghar ka zayqa—so every bottle feels like a trusted part of the kitchen, not just another product on the shelf.

Mission

To deliver premium mustard oil that brings purity, aroma, and confidence to everyday Pakistani cooking.

Vision

To become a household name associated with traditional taste, trusted quality, and modern presentation.

Values

Purity, family, consistency, and respect for desi culinary heritage in every decision we make.
